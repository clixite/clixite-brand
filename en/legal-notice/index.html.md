## Your personal data

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics tools and loads no external resources: the fonts are hosted on our own server. If you pause the home page video, it stores that single setting in your browser: the choice stays on your device and is never sent to us.

The server keeps technical logs (IP address, date, page requested) for a limited period, for the security of the service (legitimate interest).

If you write to us, we use your name, your email address and the content of your message to reply to you and, if you ask, to prepare a proposal (pre-contractual steps taken at your request). This data is neither sold nor passed on. Data controller: Clixite SRL.

You can request access to your data, its rectification or erasure, the restriction of its processing, or object to that processing, by writing to [nicolas@clixite.be](mailto:nicolas@clixite.be). You can also lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority, Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels ([dataprotectionauthority.be](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be/)).
