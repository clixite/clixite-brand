# Programme management: multi-team programmes kept on track through to go-live.

Programme management coordinates several projects that serve a single objective, covering governance, planning, budget, risk and change management. Clixite launches or takes over your transformation programmes in Belgium and Luxembourg, with recognised frameworks (PMP, PRINCE2, SAFe, Scrum, ADKAR) and one rule: decisions are made on the evidence.

Updated 26 October 2026

## When to bring us in

- A programme has fallen behind or its budget is drifting.
- Several teams and several suppliers have to move at the same pace.
- Committees meet but do not decide.
- A critical go-live is approaching.
- Users are not adopting what has been delivered.

## What you get

**A recovery assessment**
Where the programme really stands: scope, schedule, budget, risks and dependencies.

**Governance that decides**
Clear committees, roles and decision rules, and dashboards that support decisions.

**A plan that holds**
Milestones, dependencies and risks followed closely, with every team and every supplier.

**Change you can measure**
Training, communication and adoption tracked against indicators defined at the start.

## Frequently asked questions.

**Can you take over a programme already under way?**
Yes, this is common: an assessment, a recovery plan and clear trade-off decisions, then programme management close to the teams.

**Iterative methods or the V-model?**
Both, depending on the nature of the programme and your constraints: SAFe and Scrum for product and software, PRINCE2 or PMP for heavily governed programmes, often a deliberate mix.

**Do you work with our suppliers and integrators?**
Yes. We coordinate internal teams and suppliers, with clear commitments for each of them.

**How do you measure adoption?**
With indicators defined at the start: actual usage, support requests, quality of the data entered, user feedback.
