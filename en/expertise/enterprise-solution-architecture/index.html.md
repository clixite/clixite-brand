# Enterprise and solution architecture: decide where your IT landscape is heading.

Enterprise architecture links strategy to information systems: what you have, what you are aiming for and the path between the two. Solution architecture selects and designs the tools that get you there. Clixite does both, from mapping to production, for SMEs and regulated groups alike, in Belgium and Luxembourg.

Updated 26 October 2026

## When to bring us in

- Your applications have piled up and no one has the full picture any more.
- A major choice is coming up: ERP, CRM, procurement tool, cloud, data platform.
- A merger, a demerger or a reorganisation redraws the map.
- A regulator or an auditor asks for a clear view of your systems and your critical suppliers.
- An AI project has to find its place in the existing landscape.

## Frequently asked questions.

**What is the difference between enterprise architecture and solution architecture?**
Enterprise architecture decides on the whole picture: business, data, applications, technology, and the path from today to the target. Solution architecture designs a specific solution within that framework, down to integrations and security.

**Does an SME need enterprise architecture?**
Yes, at its own scale. A clear map of your tools and flows avoids redundant purchases and prepares for growth. For an SME, the exercise fits into a few short deliverables.

**Do you follow a specific framework?**
We draw on the concepts of recognised frameworks when they help, without imposing heavy formalism: an architecture deliverable has to be read and used by your teams.

**Can you also build the target?**
Yes, through Forward Deployed Engineering: we join your teams to take what has been decided into production, then hand it over.
