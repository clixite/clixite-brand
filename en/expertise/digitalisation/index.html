Process digitalisation: from paper and double data entry to connected tools.

Process digitalisation replaces paper, scattered files and double data entry with digital workflows and connected data. Clixite maps and simplifies your processes before automating them, then takes them into production with your teams, from SMEs to large enterprises, in Belgium and Luxembourg.

Updated 26 October 2026

When to bring us in

  • A process still runs on paper, in Excel or by email.
  • The same data is entered several times in several tools.
  • Approvals get lost between inboxes.
  • Dashboards exist but nobody uses them.
  • You want to use AI but do not know where to start.

What you get

Simplified processes
Mapped, stripped of unnecessary steps, with clear rules, before any tool is added.
Automated workflows
Forms, approvals, notifications and integrations with your CRM and your ERP.
Connected data
Data entered once, clean master data and dashboards that get used.
Applied AI
Where it saves time, under human oversight and in line with the GDPR.

Frequently asked questions.

Where should you start?
Start with the most painful and most frequent process. Simplify it before adding tools: automating a muddled process only makes it go faster.
Do you need to change tools?
Not necessarily. We first make the most of what you already have; a new tool is only justified if it does better at a reasonable cost.
What role does AI play?
Reading documents, sorting requests, drafting replies: AI is genuinely useful there, under human oversight and in line with the GDPR.
Will your teams be able to change the workflows themselves?
Yes. Documentation and training are part of every assignment, so that your teams stay self-sufficient.
