## Frequently asked questions.

**What is a Fractional CPO?**
A Fractional CPO is an experienced Chief Procurement Officer who works for your company on a shared, part-time basis, at the pace you set. You gain access to large-enterprise expertise without creating a full-time position, and you adjust the time commitment to the workload.

**Is Source-to-Pay useful for an SME?**
Yes, provided you deal with processes before tools. An SME mainly gains visibility over its spend, better negotiated terms and faster invoice processing. The tool comes afterwards, sized to your volumes.

**How does the DORA Regulation affect procurement?**
Since 17 January 2025, DORA has required financial entities in the European Union to keep a register of information on their ICT third-party service providers, assess their concentration risk and include specific clauses in their contracts. Procurement is on the front line for collecting this data and negotiating these clauses.
