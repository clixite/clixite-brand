# Five expertise areas, one standard: what we deliver has to run.

We step in where a strategy has to become a working system, in SMEs and regulated groups alike, in Belgium and Luxembourg.

1. ## [Forward Deployed Engineering](https://www.clixite.be/en/forward-deployed-engineering/)
We join your teams to design and build the integration, automation or AI agent you need and take it into production, using your tools and following your security rules. We leave when your teams run the solution on their own.

  - A prototype proven on your real data
  - A solution in production, monitored and documented
  - A complete handover: code, access, documentation, trained teams
Systems integration, process automation, AI agents, go-live, handover
[Forward Deployed Engineering in detail](https://www.clixite.be/en/forward-deployed-engineering/)
2. ## [GEO: visibility in AI assistants](https://www.clixite.be/en/generative-engine-optimization/)
When your clients ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini or Copilot who can help them, your company needs to be part of the answer. We make your offering understandable, verifiable and citable by answer engines as well as by Google.

  - An audit of your visibility in AI assistants, question by question
  - Consistent pages, structured data and entity details that engines understand
  - An aligned off-site presence and citation tracking
GEO, SEO, structured data, Wikidata, Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools
[GEO in detail](https://www.clixite.be/en/generative-engine-optimization/)
3. ## [Enterprise and solution architecture](https://www.clixite.be/en/expertise/enterprise-solution-architecture/)
Know what you have, decide where you are going and choose the solutions that take you there, without piling up tools.

  - Mapping of applications, data and flows
  - Target architecture and roadmap, as costed options
  - Solution selection and external architecture reviews
IT landscape mapping, target architecture, integration, cloud
[Architecture in detail](https://www.clixite.be/en/expertise/enterprise-solution-architecture/)
4. ## [Programme management](https://www.clixite.be/en/expertise/programme-management/)
Multi-team programmes scoped and kept on track: clear governance, risks tracked, committees that decide, users on board from day one.

  - Plan, budget and risks back under control
  - Governance and committees that make decisions
  - Change management you can measure
PMP, PRINCE2, SAFe, Scrum, ADKAR
[Programme management in detail](https://www.clixite.be/en/expertise/programme-management/)
5. ## [Digitalisation](https://www.clixite.be/en/expertise/digitalisation/)
Paper processes, double data entry and scattered files become connected tools that work for your teams.

  - Processes mapped and simplified before any tool is added
  - Automated forms, workflows and approvals
  - Data connected across your tools, dashboards that get used
Process automation, CRM and ERP, data, applied AI
[Digitalisation in detail](https://www.clixite.be/en/expertise/digitalisation/)
