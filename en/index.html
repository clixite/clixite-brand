Forward Deployed Engineering in detail

  • Inside your teams, not alongside them.

    On site or remotely, in your tools and at your pace. Decisions are made where the work gets done.

  • In production, not in PowerPoint.

    The assignment is measured by what runs: an integration, an automated process, an AI agent in use on Monday morning.

  • Under your control, then in your hands.

    Your environments, your security rules, named access. At the end, the code, the documentation and the know-how stay with you.

GEO: be the answer AI assistants give.

Your clients no longer just search Google. They ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini or Copilot who can help them. We work to help assistants find you and describe you accurately.

  1. Measure

    The questions your clients ask, tested on the main assistants: who gets cited, and with what information.

  2. Structure

    A consistent entity, pages that answer questions and accurate structured data: your site becomes citable.

  3. Publish and monitor

    Business listings, profiles, open databases, third-party mentions, then regular tests: assistants cross-check their sources before they cite.
