# Consulting and engineering since 2005.

Clixite is a Belgian consulting and engineering company based in Wavre. It offers five expertise areas, from target to production, and a dedicated procurement transformation offer, for growing SMEs and regulated groups alike.

Its rule fits in one sentence: “We solve your x.” The problem that holds things up, the one no one has named yet.
