## From discovery to go-live.

Four stages, each with a deliverable you keep, even if you stop there. You decide on the next one based on evidence.

1. **Discover**
A map of your systems, flows, contracts and risks. We measure before we draw.
Deliverable: Assessment
2. **Design**
The target architecture and the path to get there, as costed options. What we keep, what we replace, what we stop.
Deliverable: Target architecture and roadmap
3. **Build**
Integrations, data, automations and AI, delivered in short increments that your teams test in real conditions.
Deliverable: Increments in production
4. **Go live**
Deployment, adoption and handover. We leave when your teams run the solution on their own.
Deliverable: Solution in operation and documented
